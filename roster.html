Champions

Every champion in Burizza with their three active skills and their own passive. Every champion also has Attack and Guard.

  • CD = cooldown in turns
  • ★ = signature, unlocks on the 3rd turn
  • Round icon = passive (some champions have two to choose from)
  • Loadout: equip 2 actives + 1 passive (more slots at Lv 5, 10, 15)
