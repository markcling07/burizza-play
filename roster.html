Champions

Every champion in Burizza with all of their skills, their own passive (and any alternatives), and their Nature. Every champion also has Attack and Guard.

  • CD = cooldown in turns
  • ★ = signature, unlocks on the 3rd turn
  • Round icon = passive (some champions have alternatives to equip instead)
  • Nature = what no training changes: always on, takes no slot, nothing swaps it away
  • Loadout: 6 active slots + 2 passive slots at every level; champions start at level 15
